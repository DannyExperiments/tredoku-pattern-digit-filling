Every tredoku pattern can be filled

Each diamond is split into a 3×3 grid. The rule below puts 1–9 in every diamond and in every line of nine cells that follows a run of three diamonds, even where the line bends. Drag to rotate; click a cell.

Drag to rotate · click a cell to show its diamond and its lines

How the digits are chosen

Give each of the three edge directions a fixed orientation. A cell's position along a direction is then the same on both sides of every shared edge. Attach the forms x, y, x+y (mod 3) to the three directions, and give the three diamonds of each run different offsets 0, 1, 2. The cell at position (i, j) gets the pair (x, y) with formd = i + offset and forme = j + offset, written as the digit 1 + 3x + y.
