# Changes from the canonical proof note
The mathematics is unchanged. Changes are expository only:
- Coordinates written as sub-diamonds T(i,j) of P + [0,1]u_d + [0,1]u_e; the index-preservation step is a separate lemma (Lemma 2.1) with an explicit proof.
- The run-structure argument (exactly two d-sides, at most one neighbour per side, no cycles by a monotone transverse coordinate) is a separate lemma (Lemma 2.3).
- "At most one run" changed to "exactly one run" (possibly of length 1).
- "Follows by subtraction" replaced by the determinants ±1.
- Definition 2.2 makes "line" precise; lines may bend.
- The q×q statement is restricted to runs of length exactly q (runs of length ≤ q get distinct labels).
- States that non-overlap is the only structural hypothesis used.
- Prior-art remark and citations added (ribbons, Latin squares, design keys, Ridout).
