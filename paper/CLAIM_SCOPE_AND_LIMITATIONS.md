# Claim scope and limitations
- Proved: every tredoku pattern admits a filling (Blackburn, EJC 33(3) 2026 #P3.58, concluding remarks item 2), and more generally Theorem 3.1 for every q ≥ 2 and every finite set of non-overlapping lozenges.
- Existence only: nothing about puzzles with givens or unique solvability.
- "Straight line" is read as the strand that crosses the run's shared edges. In mixed runs it bends; a strictly Euclidean reading only removes constraints.
- Lean: partial logical-core check only (see formalization/).
- Not refereed. Novelty: apparently new after a documented search through 2026-11-26; the ingredients are standard.
